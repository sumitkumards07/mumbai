Dharmik Standards & Modern Hygiene

Thoughtfully Crafted For Every Yatri

Every facility at Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan is designed to protect your spiritual routine without compromising on everyday comfort.

100% Satvik Purity

Our entire premises are strictly pure vegetarian. Consuming or bringing non-vegetarian food, eggs, alcohol, or tobacco is strictly forbidden.

Jain Bhojanshala Proximity

Several renowned Jain Bhojanalays serving satvik, chauvihar-compliant meals are located within 2 to 5 minutes walking distance in Chira Bazar.

24/7 Hot Water

Continuous hot water is available in all attached private bathrooms and hall washrooms, ensuring refreshing morning snan before jin-pooja.

Boiled Drinking Water

Filtered and boiled drinking water (Ubaala hua paani) is readily provided on request for yatris observing specific religious dietary disciplines.

Air-Conditioned Comfort

Modern, silent split air-conditioners are installed in our Super Delux rooms, 6-bed rooms, and large AC halls to beat Mumbai's coastal humidity.

24x7 CCTV & Security

Complete CCTV monitoring across all common corridors and entrances, backed by attentive staff to ensure safety for families and solo female yatris.

Elderly & Senior Care

Ground-level room access, comfortable Western washrooms, and staff assistance for elderly yatris visiting South Mumbai for medical or temple purposes.

Secure Luggage Storage

Safe luggage holding area available for guests arriving early from outstation trains or exploring Mumbai after checking out before late night departure.

24-Hour Front Desk

Our reception operates around the clock to welcome yatris arriving on midnight trains at Mumbai Central or CSMT and provide instant assistance.
