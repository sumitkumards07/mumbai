Welcome to Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan

A Sacred Sanctuary in South Mumbai

Rooted in Peace, Purity & Warm Jain Hospitality

Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan is a serene and deeply spiritual sanctuary located in the bustling heart of South Mumbai, specifically in Zaobawadi, Chira Bazar, near the famous Ram Mandir in Thakurdwar. Established with the noble intention of serving the Jain community, yatris (pilgrims), and respectful travelers, our Dharamshala provides authentic purity, tranquility, and traditional hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about accommodation, satvik dining, temple proximity, and booking at Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan.

Q1 What is Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan and where is it located?

Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan is a peaceful, hygienic Jain dharamshala located at 1 Zaobawadi, Chira Bazar Road, near Ram Mandir, Thakurdwar, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400002. Established to serve Jain yatris, pilgrims, families, and visitors, we provide comfortable lodging in the historic heart of South Mumbai close to major Jain Derasars and transit terminals.

Q2 What room categories and tariffs are available?

We provide six distinct accommodation options to suit families, individual pilgrims, and groups:

  • Dormitory – Bed: ₹800 / night
  • AC Hall – Bed: ₹800 / night
  • Non-AC Room: ₹1,800 / night
  • Super Deluxe Room: ₹2,400 / night
  • Super Delux Room (AC): ₹2,800 / night
  • 6 Beds AC Room: ₹3,850 / night

All rooms are clean, sanitized, and maintained under strict community hygiene standards.

Q3 How do I book a room or check live availability?

You can reserve rooms directly with our front desk reception via WhatsApp or direct telephone:

• WhatsApp: Send your dates and required room category to +91 9760933661.
• Phone: Call reception directly at +91 9760933661.
Direct booking eliminates intermediary commissions and ensures transparent, verified reservations.

Q4 Are pure vegetarian and satvik Jain meals available nearby?

Yes. Numerous authentic Jain Bhojanalays and satvik dining facilities operate within 200 to 500 meters in Chira Bazar, Thakurdwar, and Kalbadevi. Wholesome, pure satvik meals adhering strictly to Jain dietary principles (including chauvihar timings before sunset) are easily accessible on foot.

Q5 Which Jain Derasars are located within walking distance?

South Mumbai is home to some of India's most sacred Jain temples. Within walking distance or a short taxi ride, you will find:

• Shri Godiji Parshwanath Derasar: Renowned historic Derasar in Pydhonie (~1.8 km).
• Babu Amichand Panalal Adishwarji Jain Temple: Walkeshwar (~3.8 km).
• Chira Bazar & Thakurdwar Local Derasars: Multiple Swetambar and Digambar mandirs located within 200m to 800m for convenient morning and evening darshan.

Q6 How far is the Atithi Bhavan from Mumbai railway stations?

Our central South Mumbai location offers convenient access to all major railway hubs:

• Marine Lines Station: ~800 meters (10 min walk)
• Charni Road Station: ~1.1 km (15 min walk)
• CSMT Terminal: ~2.2 km (8–10 min taxi)
• Mumbai Central Station: ~3.5 km (12–15 min taxi)
• Dadar Terminus: ~8.5 km (20–25 min taxi or 12 min local train from Marine Lines)

Q7 What are the check-in and check-out policies?

Standard check-out is 10:00 AM (with 24-hour cycle arrangements available on request). Early morning check-in for yatris arriving on overnight outstation trains is accommodated whenever rooms are vacant. Please coordinate your arrival schedule with our reception in advance.

Q8 Is the Bhavan safe for families, solo women travelers, and senior citizens?

Yes, absolutely. The Bhavan operates under strict community discipline with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, a manned front reception desk, elevator access, and comfortable ground-level facilities. We prioritize the security, dignity, and peaceful rest of all guests.

Q9 What amenities are provided in the rooms?

Guests enjoy clean attached washrooms, 24/7 running hot water (geyser), clean bed linens, high-speed ceiling fans, split air-conditioning (in AC rooms and halls), power backup, pure filtered drinking water, and safe luggage storage.

Q10 Can group sanghs, yatri groups, or wedding parties reserve together?

Yes. Our spacious 6 Beds AC Rooms and large AC Hall (with individual bed setups at ₹800/night) are designed specifically for pilgrim sanghs, religious groups, and extended families traveling together. Advance reservation is strongly recommended.
