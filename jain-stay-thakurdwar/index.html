Thakurdwar: The Historic Heart of Jain Community Life in Mumbai

Thakurdwar and its adjoining areas of Chira Bazar, Zaobawadi, and Kalbadevi represent one of the oldest and most vibrant residential and commercial centers for the Jain community in Mumbai. For decades, pilgrims traveling from Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and across India have chosen Thakurdwar for their religious stays.

Located at 1 Zaobawadi, Chira Bazar Road, near Ram Mandir, Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan puts you right in the center of this sacred neighborhood. When staying with us, you can walk to your morning jin-darshan, attend lectures and religious pravachans, and access pure Jain food without needing city taxis or long commutes.

Why Yatris & Visitors Prefer Staying in Thakurdwar

  • Immediate Temple Proximity: Historic Jain Derasars and the renowned Ram Mandir are within 1 to 5 minutes walking distance.
  • Authentic Satvik Bhojanshalas: Pure vegetarian Jain dining halls offering authentic meals following chauvihar (before sunset) timings are right around the corner.
  • Safe & Peaceful Residential Lane: Zaobawadi provides a quiet, serene sanctuary away from main road vehicular noise, ideal for prayer and relaxation.
  • Minutes from Western Railway Line: Marine Lines Station (~800m) and Charni Road Station (~1.1km) allow effortless local transit across Mumbai.
  • Direct Access to Major Markets: Chira Bazar jewelry market, Zaveri Bazaar, Princess Street, and Mangaldas Market are all within walking distance.
