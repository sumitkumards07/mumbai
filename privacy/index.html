Privacy Policy & Guest Data Protection

Information on how Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan manages guest records and website communications.

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan ("we", "our", or "the Bhavan") operates this official website (https://www.kshetrapaljainatithibhavan.co.in/) to provide transparent information about our accommodations, room tariffs, and satvik facilities for Jain yatris, pilgrims, families, and visitors. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and guests.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us via telephone, WhatsApp, or in person at our front desk reception, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact Information: Your name, mobile phone number, city of origin, and email address.
  • Reservation Information: Arrival date, departure date, room category preference, and number of accompanying family members or sangh travelers.
  • Official Identification: In accordance with Indian hospitality regulations and local law enforcement guidelines, guests checking into the Dharamshala are required to present valid government-issued photo ID (such as Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) for registration in the guest ledger.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for legitimate guest management and communication purposes:

  • Confirming your room reservation and sending check-in instructions.
  • Responding to your room tariff, availability, and transit queries on WhatsApp or telephone.
  • Fulfilling legal guest register recording requirements under applicable local laws.
  • Ensuring the safety, security, and peaceful environment of the Dharamshala premises.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal contact details with commercial marketing agencies, advertising networks, or third-party lead brokers. Information is disclosed only to law enforcement authorities if officially mandated by legal order or statutory regulations.

5. Security & CCTV Surveillance

For the safety of all pilgrims, families, and resident guests, the common areas and entrance passages of Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan are monitored by 24/7 CCTV surveillance cameras. CCTV recordings are retained for security purposes and are not made public.

6. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or wish to update your reservation details, please contact our reception:
