Sacred Jain Derasars & Religious Places

Ram Mandir, Thakurdwar

~50 meters

Historic spiritual landmark located directly adjacent to our lane in Zaobawadi. Prominent landmark used by taxi drivers and local pilgrims.

1 min walk View Map

Chira Bazar Jain Derasar

~200 meters

Beautiful, serene local Jain temple offering regular morning jin-pooja, darshan, and spiritual pravachans for community members and yatris.

2-3 mins walk View Map

Godiji Parshwanath Jain Temple, Pydhonie

~1.8 km

One of Mumbai’s most revered and historic Jain Derasars, dedicated to Lord Parshwanath. Renowned for its intricate woodwork and deep spiritual vibration.

8-10 mins drive View Map

Babu Amichand Panalal Adishwarji Jain Temple

~3.8 km

Famous Walkeshwar / Malabar Hill Jain temple known for its breathtaking marble carvings, vibrant astrological ceiling frescos, and peaceful hillside atmosphere.

12-15 mins drive View Map

Mumbadevi Temple, Bhuleshwar

~1.2 km

The historic patron goddess temple of Mumbai situated in the bustling traditional streets of Bhuleshwar, easily reachable on foot or by short auto/taxi.

12-15 mins walk View Map

Motisha Jain Derasar, Byculla

~4.2 km

Magnificent 19th-century white marble Jain temple with grand courtyards and shrines, a primary stop on South Mumbai Chaitya Paripati pilgrimages.

15 mins drive View Map

Railway Stations & Transit Hubs

Marine Lines Railway Station

~800 meters

Nearest railway station on the Western Suburban Line. Effortless 8 to 10-minute walk down Princess Street to reach our guest house.

8-10 mins walk View Map

Charni Road Railway Station

~1.1 km

Major suburban station on the Western Line near Girgaon Chowpatty. Very convenient for guests arriving via local trains from Borivali/Bandra.

12 mins walk / 3 mins taxi View Map

CSMT (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus)

~2.2 km

UNESCO World Heritage terminal connecting Mumbai to all major Indian cities via Central Railway outstation express trains.

10 mins taxi View Map

Mumbai Central Railway Station

~3.5 km

Primary western railway terminal for Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Vande Bharat trains arriving from Gujarat, Delhi, and Rajasthan.

15 mins taxi View Map

Wholesale Markets & Commercial Hubs

Chira Bazar Market

~100 meters

Traditional market famous for jewelry, bridal silverware, silk fabrics, pooja articles, and daily satvik grocery provisions.

2 mins walk View Map

Zaveri Bazaar

~1.3 km

India’s largest gold, diamond, and precious gem trading hub. Essential destination for traders, jewelers, and wedding shoppers staying with us.

12-14 mins walk View Map

Mangaldas & Mulji Jetha (MJ) Market

~1.1 km

Asia’s premier wholesale textile markets offering endless selections of sarees, dress materials, home linen, and festive clothing.

10-12 mins walk View Map

Crawford Market (Mahatma Jyotiba Phule)

~1.6 km

Iconic historic wholesale market for spices, dry fruits, gift hampers, confectionery, household items, and travel accessories.

6-8 mins taxi View Map

~1.2 km

Premier multi-specialty hospital located at Charni Road. Our Bhavan offers peaceful, affordable lodging for patients' attending families.

Bombay Hospital & Medical Research Centre

Renowned medical institution situated near Marine Lines. Conveniently close for families visiting specialists in South Mumbai.
