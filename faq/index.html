1. Reservations, Pricing & Check-in

What are the room tariffs at Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan?

Our standard nightly tariffs are: Dormitory Bed (₹800), AC Hall Bed (₹800), Non-AC Room (₹1,800), Super Deluxe Room (₹2,400), Super Delux Room - AC (₹2,800), and 6 Beds AC Room (₹3,850). Rates are fixed with no hidden fees.

How do I reserve a room in advance?

You can book directly by calling our reception at +91 9760933661 or sending a WhatsApp message. We confirm availability immediately and guide you through the check-in documentation.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Our reception operates 24 hours to accommodate guests arriving on late-night or early-morning trains. Please inform us about your travel schedule so our staff can keep your room prepared upon arrival.

2. Dharmik Culture & Satvik Food Facilities

Are pure Jain meals available nearby?

Yes, several authentic Jain Bhojanalays are located within a 2 to 5-minute walk along Chira Bazar and Thakurdwar. They serve freshly cooked, pure satvik meals following sunset (chauvihar) disciplines and morning navkarishi rules.

Is boiled drinking water available?

Yes, boiled drinking water (ubaala hua paani) is readily provided on request for yatris observing specific religious dietary disciplines or fasting.

3. Temple Access & Neighborhood Guidance

Which Jain Derasars are within walking distance?

Ram Mandir Thakurdwar is just ~50 meters away (1 minute walk). Chira Bazar Jain Derasar is ~200 meters away (2-3 minutes walk). Other historic shrines like Godiji Parshwanath Jain Temple Pydhonie and Babu Amichand Panalal Derasar Walkeshwar are just a short taxi ride away.

Which is the closest railway station?

Marine Lines Station on the Western line is only ~800 meters away (8-10 minutes walking). Charni Road Station is ~1.1 km away. CSMT is ~2.2 km (10 mins by taxi) and Mumbai Central is ~3.5 km (15 mins by taxi).

4. Facilities, Security & Elderly Comfort

Do the rooms have 24-hour hot water?

Yes, hot water is provided round-the-clock across all attached private bathrooms, hall facilities, and dormitories.

Is luggage storage available after checkout?

Yes, we provide secure temporary luggage storage free of charge for registered guests who wish to visit Derasars or markets before boarding late-night trains.

Is the property safe for families and solo female yatris?

Extremely safe. Zaobawadi is a peaceful, traditional residential enclave. The Bhavan features 24-hour CCTV coverage, locked gates at night, and courteous staff available 24/7.
