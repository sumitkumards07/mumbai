A Peaceful Yatri Niwas in the Heart of South Mumbai

Finding safe, serene, and culturally authentic Jain accommodation in Mumbai can often be challenging in a bustling metropolis. Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan was established to provide Jain yatris, pilgrim families, and respectful travelers with clean, spiritually aligned lodging at very affordable tariffs.

Situated at 1 Zaobawadi, Chira Bazar Road, near Ram Mandir, Thakurdwar, our Atithi Bhavan is located in one of Mumbai's most culturally rich Jain neighborhoods. Whether you are arriving for temple darshan, Chaitya Paripati, business in South Mumbai wholesale markets, or medical visits at top hospitals, our property offers a tranquil home-away-from-home.

Adherence to Jain Values & Dharmik Culture

Unlike commercial hotels, Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan strictly preserves the sanctity of Jain traditions. Guests can rest assured that:

  • 100% Pure Vegetarian Premises: Non-vegetarian food, eggs, alcohol, and smoking are strictly forbidden on the premises.
  • Proximity to Jain Bhojanshalas: Authentic satvik Jain Bhojanalays serving pure Jain meals following chauvihar (sunset) timings are within a 2-5 minute walk in Chira Bazar and Thakurdwar.
  • Boiled Drinking Water: Filtered and warm/boiled drinking water is available upon request for yatris observing religious vows.
  • Peaceful Atmosphere: Quiet environment conducive to morning samayik, pratikraman, and daily prayers.

Convenient Transportation & Access in Mumbai

Reaching Shree Kshetrapal Jain Atithi Bhavan from major transport terminals is fast and convenient:

  • Marine Lines Railway Station: Only ~800 meters away (8-10 minutes walking). Easiest station for local Western line suburban trains.
  • Charni Road Railway Station: ~1.1 km away (12 minutes walk or 3 minutes taxi).
  • CSMT (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus): ~2.2 km away (10 minutes by taxi). Ideal for Central Railway and outstation trains.
  • Mumbai Central Station: ~3.5 km away (15 minutes by taxi). Direct access for Western Railway outstation trains.
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM): ~21 km (approx 45-60 minutes via Western Express Highway or Coastal Road).
