Step-by-Step Transit Directions to Our Bhavan

From Marine Lines Railway Station (~800 meters)

Exit Marine Lines station on the East side (towards Princess Street / Chira Bazar). Walk straight along Princess Street towards the Chira Bazar jewelry market. Turn towards Zaobawadi lane near the historic Ram Mandir. Total walking time is only 8 to 10 minutes.

From Charni Road Railway Station (~1.1 km)

Exit on the East side (Opera House / Girgaon). Take a short 3-minute taxi ride to "Ram Mandir, Thakurdwar / Chira Bazar" or walk eastwards down JSS Road into Zaobawadi. Fare is by regular meter taxi.

From CSMT Station (~2.2 km)

Exit CSMT towards Nagar Chowk / D.N. Road. Hire a black-and-yellow taxi to "Zaobawadi, Chira Bazar, Thakurdwar near Ram Mandir". Travel time is roughly 10 minutes depending on daytime traffic.

From Mumbai Central Station (~3.5 km)

Take a direct taxi heading south via Maharshi Karve Road or Tardeo Road towards Thakurdwar. Inform the driver: "Thakurdwar, Chira Bazar, near Ram Mandir". Travel time is approximately 15 minutes.

From Mumbai International Airport (BOM) (~21 km)

Book an airport prepaid or app-based cab (Uber/Ola) towards South Mumbai. Take the Western Express Highway / Coastal Road exit towards Marine Drive / Princess Street flyover, turning into Chira Bazar. Total travel time is 45 to 60 minutes.
